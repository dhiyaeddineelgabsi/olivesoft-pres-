04

The n8n
workflow

29 workflows
Durable job queue
Automatic recovery
[0:30] n8n is the heart of the system: twenty-nine workflows. This one is the job runner. Every minute it claims the next job from a durable queue in Postgres, with a lease, so two workers never take the same job. Each stage saves a checkpoint, and when something fails it retries automatically, up to three times, without redoing the work already done.

[0:40] It starts with collection. Every morning at six, this workflow reads four official sources: TED, for Europe and France, and BOAMP, both through their APIs; Find a Tender, in the UK, through its OCDS API; and HAICOP, in Tunisia, which we scrape from its public pages. An LLM extracts the requirements, and we enforce one rule. (click) Every requirement must come with an exact quote from the notice, or it is dropped. The notice is treated as data, never as instructions. A notice with no deadline or no usable requirement is held for human review, never silently lost.

[0:55] Then the core of the matching: RAG over our own knowledge. Every document, a CV, a project reference, a tech stack, is extracted, cut into chunks, turned into Gemini vectors and stored in Qdrant. For each requirement of the tender, we retrieve the top five passages, and an LLM judges it: supported, partial, unsupported or unknown, always with the quote that proves it. (click) Here, the requirement "Data Scientist role" is supported, and you can read the exact line from the CV. (click) We benchmarked it on labelled queries: hit at five is one point zero, recall at five is zero point eight three, and eighty-three percent of requirement judgments are correct. When the evidence is missing, the system says unknown. It never invents a qualification.

[0:30] When the team accepts an opportunity, two AI agents take over: a strategist plans the proposal, and a writer drafts it. Every claim is checked against its source before it can reach a slide. The renderer then produces a real, editable PowerPoint, and a PDF. Anything we cannot prove becomes a yellow field, for the team to complete.

[0:20] Security was built in from day one. API keys stay on the server. Every webhook is protected. Supabase enforces row level security. Files live in private storage, behind links that expire after sixty seconds. And every quote is verified against its source, so text injected into a web page cannot slip into a proposal.
