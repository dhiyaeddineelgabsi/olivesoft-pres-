29 workflows

[0:25] n8n is the heart of the system: twenty-nine workflows. This one is the job runner. Every minute it claims the next job from a durable queue in Postgres, with a lease, so no job runs twice. If a stage fails, it retries up to three times, from its last checkpoint.
